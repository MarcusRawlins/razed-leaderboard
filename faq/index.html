FAQ

Good questions. Straight answers.

What is Harmonic Energy?+

It’s our song. We wrote it in the 1990s by turning molecular measurements into notes on the twelve-tone scale. Everything we make carries it.

What does it actually do?+

Products carrying Harmonic Energy help your body’s natural energy field stay balanced while you use them. We don’t claim to diagnose, treat or cure anything.

How do I know if it’s working for me?+

Run the experiment. Take the Snapshot, use a kit every day for 30 days, then take it again and compare. Your results are the only review that counts.

Do I need to charge anything?+

Nope. No batteries, no apps, no Bluetooth pairing. Cells and Wave Riders are passive tags, and Liquilight is a liquid.

Can my dog get in on this? My ficus?+

Absolutely. The A-series is made for animals and the P-series for plants. For a specific health concern, ask your vet.

Where else can I buy it?+

Partner brands, including Life Armor and NanoVibe, build it into their own products. Their stores set their own terms.

I make products. Can I add it to mine?+

That’s our favorite question. Tell us what you make, and we’ll pick a format, send samples and quote a license. Most of our work happens inside other people’s brands.
