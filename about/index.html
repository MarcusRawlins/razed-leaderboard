Our story

It started with a violin and a weird question.

The idea

Everything has a rhythm.

It’s an old idea: everything, down to the atom, is energy in motion, and living things fall into step with the rhythms around them. We wanted to make that idea useful.

We pictured a belt, a T-shirt, a pendant and a bottle of shampoo, each carrying the same music. Then we spent two decades building them.

The music

The first product was an album.

Co-developer Robert Anthony Aviles, a composer on 7-string electric violin, turned the measurements into music worth listening to. Rhythm of the Light came out in 1998, after an early trial in a hospital emergency department.

Here he plays one of those early pieces, Tibet, live.

Tibet, performed live by Robert Anthony Aviles

Our mission

Turn down the noise. Turn up the music.

Today most of our work happens behind other people’s brands, in products made by companies, clinics and households around the world. We license the technology for consumables, cosmetics, apparel and jewelry, and we sell our own kits and the Chromatones membership directly.

Helping people worldwide, one person at a time, since 1998.
