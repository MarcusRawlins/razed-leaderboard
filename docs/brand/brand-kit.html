sonaura

One original piece of music from 1998, built into things you wear, add and carry, and licensed to the brands that make them.

i.

Name

Sonaura so-NOR-ah

Sono, sound, plus aura, the field around you. It says what the company does in one word: it takes a piece of music and puts it into something you carry. It's a coined word, so it can be owned. A first web search found no wellness, jewelry or cosmetics brand using it.

Before launch: a USPTO search in classes 3, 9, 14 and 41, the .com (fallback: sonaura.co), and matching social handles.

ii.

Promise

Tune your day.

Short, active, and it works on two levels: tuning, as in the music, and getting your day back into balance. It turns every product into a small daily ritual: put on the pendant, add the drops, take the Snapshot, play a program.

Positioning

For people who want calm, steady everyday energy, and for the brands that make the things those people already use. Sonaura is a sound studio that turns one original composition into objects, liquids and signals you can carry.

Why us

Frequency gadgets lead with mechanisms and big promises. Sonaura starts from real music, written in 1998 and played on a 7-string electric violin, and makes one modest, specific claim.

Kept from the original site as the brand line: Helping people worldwide, one person at a time, since 1998.

iii.

Voice

Warm, plain and specific. We sound like a musician explaining their craft, not a lab or a guru.

We are

  • Curious and grounded
  • Specific: dates, drops, ounces
  • Modest about outcomes
  • Musical in metaphor

We are not

  • Mystical or preachy
  • Vague ("amazing results")
  • Promising cures, shields or proof
  • Pseudo-technical

iv.

Logo

A ring for the field, with a single wave of sound passing through it, beside a lowercase serif wordmark. The wave always stays brass.

Primary, on Ivory

Reversed, on Midnight

Mark alone: app icon, favicon, packaging seal

Minimum size: mark 20 px, full logo 96 px wide. Clear space equals the ring's height. Never stretch it, recolor it, or place it on a busy photo without a Midnight scrim. Files: public/brand/ in the repo. The wordmark should be outlined by a designer before print.

vii.

Imagery

Shoot

  • Real hands with real products
  • Natural light and warm grain
  • The violin, strings and sound waves as recurring motifs
  • Everyday rituals: morning pendant, drops in a bottle

Avoid

  • Glowing auras and energy swirls
  • Lab coats and fake clinical settings
  • Stock "zen" clichés
  • Before-and-after body shots

ix.

The one claim

Always paired with the disclaimer

Products carrying Harmonic Energy help the body's natural energy field hold a balanced, steady state while the product is present.

Everything else is story, craft and ritual. Payment processors and ad platforms review health language, so keeping to this one claim protects checkout as well as the brand. Pending counsel sign-off.
