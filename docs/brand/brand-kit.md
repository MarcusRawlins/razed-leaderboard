# Sonaura brand kit

Working name, pending a trademark and domain check (see Name). Everything below is built so the name can change in one place: `lib/brand.ts`.

## Name

**Sonaura** (so-NOR-ah). *Sono*, sound, plus *aura*, the field around you. It says what the company does: it turns a piece of music into something you carry. It is a coined word, so it can be owned, and no conflicting wellness, jewelry or cosmetics brand turned up in a first web search.

| Option | Idea | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sonaura** (recommended) | Sound + aura | Coined, ownable, easy to say. No conflicts found in a first search |
| Cadenza & Co. | The solo passage in a concerto, often improvised by the violinist | Nods to the violin origin. "Cadenza" alone is widely used (cars, software), so it is weak as a trademark |
| Tonefield | A tone carried into your field | Plain and descriptive. Harder to trademark |
| Harmona | From "harmony" | Close to Harmoniq (a QNET wellness patch line), so confusion risk |
| Overture | The opening of a piece | Strong word, but common in many classes |

Before launch: run a USPTO search in classes 3 (cosmetics), 14 (jewelry), 9 (digital audio) and 41 (entertainment/music), and buy the .com, or else get.sonaura.com or sonaura.co as a fallback, plus matching social handles.

## Positioning

**For** people who want calm, steady everyday energy, and for the brands that make the things those people already use.

**Sonaura is** a sound studio that turns one original composition into objects, liquids and signals you can carry.

**Unlike** frequency gadgets that lead with mechanism and big promises, Sonaura starts from real music, written in 1998 and played on a 7-string electric violin, and makes a modest, specific claim.

**Promise:** *Tune your day.* Short, active, and a double meaning: the music, and getting yourself back into balance. It pairs with the daily rituals (the pendant, the drops, the Snapshot, Chromatones programs).

**Brand line (footer, kept from the original site):** *Helping people worldwide, one person at a time, since 1998.*

## Voice

Warm, plain, specific. We sound like a musician explaining their craft, not a lab or a guru.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Curious and grounded | Mystical or preachy |
| Specific (dates, drops, ounces) | Vague ("amazing results") |
| Modest about outcomes | Promising cures, shields or proof |
| Musical in metaphor (tune, rhythm, carry) | Pseudo-technical (quantum, entangled) |

**Words we use:** tune, carry, balance, steady, settle, rhythm, everyday, wear, add, notice, try.

**Words we never use in marketing:** cure, heal, treat, prevent, remedy, protect/shield (from EMF), detox, anti-aging, proven, clinically proven, quantum, entangled, millions.

**The one claim we make:** products carrying Harmonic Energy help your body's natural energy field stay balanced while you carry them. Always paired with the standard disclaimer (see `lib/content.ts`, `DISCLAIMER`). This still needs counsel's sign-off.

## Logo

A ring (the field) with a single sine wave passing through it (the tone), beside a lowercase serif wordmark. Files: `public/brand/logo.svg`, `public/brand/mark.svg`, `public/brand/logo-light.svg`.

- Minimum size: mark 20 px; full logo 96 px wide.
- Clear space: the height of the ring on all sides.
- Never stretch it, recolor it outside the palette, or set it on busy photos without the Midnight scrim.

## Type

- **Display:** Fraunces (Google Fonts), 400–600 weight, slight optical softness. Headlines and pull quotes.
- **Text:** DM Sans (Google Fonts), 400/500/700. Body, UI, buttons.
- Scale: 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 28 / 40 / 56 / 72 px. Body copy at 18 px, line height 1.6.

## Imagery

Real hands, real products, natural light, warm grain. The violin, strings and sound waves as recurring motifs. No glowing auras, no lab coats, no stock "energy" swirls. Until the client supplies photos, the site uses abstract wave graphics drawn in code.
